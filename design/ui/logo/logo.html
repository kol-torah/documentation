Clear space & minimum size

Clear space on all sides equals the width of one ruled line inside the mark (about 18% of mark height). Minimum sizes: mark 20px tall on screen, 6mm in print; lockup 120px wide. Below 20px use favicon.svg, which drops to two rules and an outlined arch on a square tile.

Don't

Don't recolour the arch. Green or one ink only; gold belongs to the third rule.
Don't add a second gold element — the mark has exactly one.
Don't outline, shadow, or gradient it.
Don't stretch, rotate, or reproportion the arch.
Don't place the solid mark on a mid-tone photo; use mark-reverse.svg on green.
Don't set the wordmark in any face other than Noto Serif Hebrew 700.
Don't use mark-mono.svg through an <img> tag — currentColor only resolves when the SVG is inlined. Use mark-black.svg or mark-white.svg there.
