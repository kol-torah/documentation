Turn 4 — fixing the green/gold
Green and gold, three resolutions
The 3b problem: mid-lightness cool green + warm yellow gold, similar chroma — they fight for the same rank. 4a warms the gold to brass so both sit on the same side. 4b drops the dark band entirely and makes green the ink, champagne the highlight. 4c takes the green almost to black and uses gold only as a line, never a fill — gilt on a binding.
4a
Pine & brass
Green pushed deep and slightly warm; gold pulled off yellow toward brass (hue 72). Both now read warm, so the gold sits on the green instead of beside it. Frank Ruhl Libre + Assistant.

Turn 3 — gold kept, blue dropped, same lesson page
Three ways to keep the gold
Gold stays as the live/active color in all three. What changes is the color it sits against — and the type pairing with it. 3a ink-and-gold, 3b deep green, 3c gold alone on cream. Grounds are pulled off the red side: neutral-to-cool-warm, no terracotta.
